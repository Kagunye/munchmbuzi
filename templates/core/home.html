Feeling hungry?

Come and dine

We serve a variety of delicious flame-grilled meats: chicken, beef and goat from sustainable and organic suppliers, and free-range fresh fish (tilapia) from Lake Victoria. All our food is professionally butchered.

Before being flame grilled, our T-bones, chickens, ribs and legs are infused in Mbuzi Munch's secret blend of herbs and sauces for an authentic taste of the Kenyan national dish.

  • ≤ 13 kgFor extra tenderness we serve younger animals. For the goats this means up to a year old.
  • MaasaiThe goat is completely free range and farmed by the Maasai.
  • L. VictoriaFree-range fresh tilapia, fried or fricassee.
  • 7 daysWe are open 10:00 – 22:00. Golf Park from 07:00.
