{# I · The pasture #}
Chapter I

The pasture

All our goats graze free range and are compassionately raised. The goat is completely free range and farmed by the Maasai.

We only serve young animals, up to a year old with a carcass weight of around 13 kg, guaranteeing the fresh flavour and tenderness of the meat. Chicken and beef come from sustainable and organic suppliers, and our free-range tilapia is fresh from Lake Victoria.

At Delamere, the model farm next door supplies vegetables, herbs and ethically raised goats, so ingredients arrive at the kitchen within minutes.
