How we do it

From the Maasai pastures to your plate

All our goats graze free range and are compassionately raised. We only serve young animals with a carcass weight of around 13 kg, thus guaranteeing the fresh flavour and tenderness of the meat.

Before the meat is barbequed, we add a special blend of herbs, spices, sauces and flavours for that authentic Kenyan Nyama Choma taste.

As all of our grilled meat dishes are prepared to order, please let your waitress know for beef or mbuzi how you would like it cooked: medium, rare or well done.

What we stand for

Good food that does good

Free range, always

Our goats are completely free range and farmed by the Maasai. At Delamere, much of our produce comes straight from the model farm next door.

Recycled & upcycled

With the exception of our state of the art kitchens, wherever possible our designers include recycled or upcycled material, such as our glassware made from bottles. Drinks and sauces are stored in recyclable containers.

Buy local, buy Kenyan

We operate a Buy Local, Buy Kenyan policy for equipment, supplies and services, using local artisans wherever possible.
